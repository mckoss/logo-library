The music player

Every program runs with the music player loaded: notes (do re mi fa sol la ti), lengths (eighth quarter half whole), octaves (low middle high) and instruments (piano, flute, banjo…), built on the package's playnote, setinstrument and settempo commands. Its source, player.lgo, ships in the package's dist/. The reference links here, as ../player/ from its own page. This page's markup:
