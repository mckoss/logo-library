Music

playnote note beats
Play a note and wait for it to finish. Notes are MIDI numbers: 60 is middle C, 61 is a half step higher, 72 is an octave higher.
playnote 60 1 playnote 64 1 playnote 67 2
setinstrument name
setinstrument number
Choose the instrument: piano, harpsichord, musicbox, organ, guitar, bass, hendrix, violin, trumpet, brass, clarinet, flute, synth, banjo, steeldrum, tom or gun, or a General MIDI program number from 0 to 127.
setinstrument "flute playnote 72 2
settempo bpm
Set the speed in beats per minute.
settempo 60 playnote 60 1

Colors

"rainbow
A color for setpencolor that goes round the rainbow: each time the pen is set to it, it takes the next color of red, orange, yellow, green, blue and purple (and red after any other color).
repeat 12 [setpencolor "rainbow fd 60 bk 60 rt 30]

Debugging

pause
Pause the program here, to look at its variables and the turtle. Then Step through it one command at a time, or Continue. The playground's Step button, and its "Break at" buttons for each procedure, pause a program too.
fd 50 pause rt 90 fd 50

The Stage

window
wrap
fence
The turtle starts in WINDOW mode here: it can go past the edges, and the stage zooms out to keep the whole drawing in view. WRAP makes it come back on the other side; FENCE stops it at the edge.
wrap repeat 20 [fd 300 rt 100]
