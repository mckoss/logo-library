Drawing Polygons

February 25, 1996 By Mike Koss
Try it Logo Playground

One of the easiest things to do in Logo is use the turtle to draw various polygons. You can notice that in order to draw a closed polygon, the turtle has to walk around the edges, eventually turning through 360 degrees before coming back home. Since the turtle makes N turns for an N-sided polygon, the size of each turn is 360/N degrees.
