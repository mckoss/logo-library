Logo Playground

By Mike Koss

Write your own Logo programs here. They’re saved in this browser, so they’ll be here next time you visit on this computer. Use Download to keep a copy or move it to another computer, and Open file to bring it back. The Examples folder has every program from the lessons; open one and use Copy to make your own version.

The playground runs jslogo, Logo in JavaScript by Joshua Bell, with music commands added for this site.
